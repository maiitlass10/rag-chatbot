# Nova Cloud Storage - FAQ

## Plans
The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage. The Pro plan costs $8/month and includes 1 TB. The Team plan costs $15 per user per month with 5 TB of pooled storage.

## Security
All files are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Two-factor authentication can be enabled from Settings > Security.

## Refunds
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the billing period.
